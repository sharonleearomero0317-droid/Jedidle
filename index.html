THE FARM JOURNAL

Update log

Every little step forward.
FIRST RELEASE · OCT 26, 2026
🌱 v1.0.0 — The Beginning

Welcome to Jedidle! Your first farm is ready.

• Added Home, Map, Credits, and Update Log tabs.

• Added a draggable 2D top-down farm map.

• Added Seed Market, Greenhouse, and Crop Selling buildings.

• Added five seeds, timed crop growth, harvesting, inventory, and selling.

• Added cash, starter funds, and local save progress.

INTERFACE UPDATE · OCT 26, 2026
🌿 v1.0.1 — Green Gradient

Earlier interface experiment with green gradient styling; superseded by v1.0.2. The farm map remained unchanged.

INTERFACE UPDATE · OCT 26, 2026
🎨 v1.0.2 — Interface Type

Removed gradient styling and added an Interface Type tab with Light mode and Dark mode choices. Your choice is saved on this device; the farm map artwork stays the same.

FARM EXPANSION · OCT 26, 2026
🌾 v1.0.3 — Crops & Upgrades

Added ten new crops, the Upgrade Lab, three permanent crop-sale bonuses, and refreshed upgrade cards. Crop sale multipliers apply to both individual sales and Sell All.

PROGRESSION UPDATE · OCT 26, 2026
🌟 v1.0.4 — Longer Growth & New Upgrades

Added five crops, slowed crop growth for a more gradual progression, added a 2× crop-sale tier, and introduced a separate 1.25× faster-growth upgrade.

MAP & INTERFACE UPDATE · OCT 26, 2026
🗺️ v1.0.5 — A Bigger, Better Map

Made the farm map a full-screen view, moved farm overview details into a compact side panel, and polished the terrain, paths, water, labels, and building cards.

GREENHOUSE UPDATE · OCT 26, 2026
🌱 v1.0.7 — Faster Growth & Planting Panels

Reduced crop growth times by 15% and redesigned the Greenhouse with a plot selector beside a dedicated seed inventory, so you choose a plot and plant from the seed list.
